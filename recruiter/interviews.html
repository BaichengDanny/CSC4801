How booking works

  • Each slot is 30 minutes and starts in the future.
  • Only applicants you move to Interviewing can book, once per application.
  • A booked slot can't be deleted, and it never shows as open again.
  • If two candidates pick the same time, the first one gets it; the other sees “Slot already booked”.
